# Face identities as a grouping signal

Dates: implemented 2026-10-21 (commit b8235da), reviewed 2026-10-22 and 2026-11-11.
Outcome: [0010](../decisions/0010-face-identities-signal.md),
[0011](../decisions/0011-unknown-face-does-not-stand-in.md),
[0012](../decisions/0012-no-face-size-filter.md).

## Why

On all five talici folders DINOv2 cannot separate "same couple, new pose" (wanted merge,
0.72–0.90 on Talici 1: 8033→8049 0.72 after 94 s, 8137→8138 0.74, 8134→8135 0.75) from
"different guest, same spot" (forbidden, 0.88 on 4a). Threshold tuning cannot fix feature overlap.

## Measurements

- **Speed (CPU, 16 cores):** 0.31 s/photo on Talici 1 (2 faces/photo), 0.42 s/photo on 4a (3.4
  faces/photo); detection 0.09 s, embedding 0.09 s per face. `det_size` 1024 found only 3 more
  faces per 50 ceremony photos for +0.11 s/photo, so 640 stays.
- **Identity threshold:** AuraFace needs cosine 0.40, not the usual 0.5 (at 0.5 the 4a bride split
  into frontal + veil/profile clusters).
- **Cluster merge:** Chinese Whispers at 0.4 kept the Talici 1 bride with heart sunglasses (228
  faces) apart from without (209): centroid similarity 0.59, while centroids of different people
  stay ≤ 0.32 ⇒ merge at ≥ 0.45.
- **Small clusters** are often one person's odd angle (groom kissing in profile = own 3-face
  identity) ⇒ only identities with ≥ 10 faces count. One-off faces are ~0.3 to every centroid, the
  same as unrelated faces, so they cannot be soft-assigned.
- **Bursts must not be blocked:** at the 4a altar the groom's face is hidden in one frame and the
  bride's in the next (9852→9853, 0.6 s), which split a 5-photo group.

## Trade-offs seen

- The relaxed look-back at 0.70 (needed for 8033→8049 at 0.72) also chains different compositions
  of one scene: Talici 1 g0077 (21 photos), 4a g0111 (altar close-ups + wide shot). Under the new
  group rule ([0014](../decisions/0014-group-definition-v2.md)) wider/tighter framing is the same
  group, so this is mostly wanted.
- `faces_same_allows_unknown: true` merges 8026–8030 (Talici 1 → 213 groups) but on 4a (→ 145)
  also joins the couple walking in (0041, two unknown guests) with the altar sequence, and the vows
  close-ups (0095–0097) with 0074/0075.
- **Structural limit:** when the guest's face is hidden in both frames of two different hugs, faces
  see `{bride}` vs `{bride}` and cannot tell the hugs apart. The VLM is the safety net.
- **Garbage identities:** blurry 24–40 px background faces can form the largest identity (T4 A =
  352 faces / 175 photos, T3 C = 163), so "A and B are the couple" is not guaranteed.
- Dancing couple T4 0625–0628 stays split: the face count varies per frame, so exact set+count
  equality never fires.
- Faces never touched no-people photos; 5 false faces in total, all singletons.

## Reviews

- **2026-10-22, the agent's review of Talici 2–4 (47 changes):** 7 good, 10 acceptable, 27 bad,
  mostly judged against the old "wide vs tight = separate" rule.
- **2026-11-11, the user's review of the same 47:** 39 good, 4 acceptable (T2 04, T3 10, 16, 17),
  4 bad: T3 09 (look-back 0.74 B&W vs colour), T3 11 (crowd, random identity subsets), T3 18
  (9552–53 added), T4 13 (0607 moved, garbage A). She rated most wide/tight look-back chains good,
  which led to the group rule change. Identities: T3 A mixed, T3 C garbage, T4 A garbage, others ok.
  Settings stay: `faces_lookback_similarity_threshold` 0.70, `min_identity_faces` 10,
  `faces_same_allows_unknown` false.

## Face-size filter experiment (2026-11-11, rejected)

`faces.identity_min_face_size: 48`: smaller faces get no identity (unknown, still counted).
Identities become clean (T4 A = bride 259 faces; garbage clusters gone). Grouping effect small and
mixed vs `groups.prefilter.json` (kept in all 5 outputs):
- fixes the user's bad T4 13 (0606+0607 together);
- regressions: 4a score 319 → 316 (0076–0079 split, 9800+9802 joined), T3 9349–9356 split in two,
  T4 first dance 0573–0588 2 → 3 groups.
- 32 and 40 px change almost nothing and do not fix 0606/0607.
- Cause: in wide shots the couple's faces are 40–48 px and lose the "same people" relaxation.

The user rejected it after seeing before/after sheets (`filter_primer_1/2`). The branch was deleted.
