# Qwen3.8-27B (4-bit) on the L4: judge, ranking, per-photo

Date: 2026-11-14 (runs 16:12 – 17:00). Follows
[ranking with the full 9B](2026-11-14-ranking-full-model-and-per-photo.md) and the
[first judge run](2026-11-11-judge-first-real-run.md). Question: does a three times larger model
of the same family do the same tasks better?

## Setup

- Server: the same `g6.xlarge` (L4 24 GB), vLLM 0.33, `RedHatAI/Qwen3.8-27B-INT4` (W4A16, vision
  tower not quantized, FP8 KV cache, 19.5 GB), `--max-model-len 8192 --max-num-seqs 2
  --gpu-memory-utilization 0.97`, thinking off, temperature 0. KV cache 25k tokens. Start notes in
  [aws-setup](../aws-setup.md).
- **Not the full model**: bf16 (~56 GB) and FP8 (~30 GB) do not fit 24 GB. A bad result here can
  be the model or the quantization.
- Data: talici 4a, default config, prompts unchanged. `config.yaml` was not changed (a copy with
  the other `vlm.model` was used).

## Results

| Task | Qwen3.5-9B | Qwen3.8-27B 4-bit | Pipeline alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Judge v1, 56 labelled neighbour pairs: correct | 48 (4-bit, T4) | **41** | 52 |
| Judge: breaks / fixes pipeline decisions | 7 / 2 | 14 / 3 | – |
| Judge: `same_group` precision / recall | 0.855 / 1.000 | 0.902 / 0.771 | 0.978 / 0.938 |
| Ranking: same winner in both orders (53 variants) | 12 (full), 15 (4-bit) | **26** | – |
| Ranking: same winner in both orders, pairs (40) | 7, 10 | 19 (a coin: ~20) | – |
| Ranking: winner = photo 1, forward run | 41 | 22 | – |
| Per photo: variants tied at the top (53) | 29 | 37 | – |

Seconds: judge 5.6 per pair (10.8 min), ranking 8.4 median (8.1 min), per photo 4.7 (9.7 min).
No request failed or was repaired.

- **Judge: worse.** 11 of the 15 errors are false splits, almost all with a reason about
  background people ("additional guests on the far right", "a priest appears"); 4 are false
  merges ("same couple, same location, different poses"). The bias moved from "same" (9B, v1
  prompt) towards "different" with the same prompt; the weakness is the same: main people vs
  background, change of action.
- **Ranking: the strong first-photo bias is gone, the answers are still not about the photos.**
  In pairs the two orders agree in 19 of 40, which is what a coin gives; of the 21 that disagree,
  13 picked their photo 1 twice and 8 their photo 2 twice. No winner has an `issue` (53 of 53
  `none`), as before.
- **Per photo: coarser, not finer.** `focus` is `sharp` for 124 of 124 and `exposure` `ok` for
  124 of 124; scores are 8 or 9 for 109 of 124. `obstructed: yes` dropped from 61 to 3 and
  `eyes: some_closed` rose from 11 to 21; which model is right is not known (no ground truth).
  The prompt was rewritten from the description in the earlier note (the original text was not
  kept), so the tie counts are not a strict like-for-like comparison.
- **Agreement between methods:** of the 26 stable ranking winners, 24 are in the per-photo top set
  (8 as the only top photo). Variants stable with both models: 7, the same winner in 5. Variants
  with one clear per-photo winner in both models: 9, the same in 7.

## Conclusion

A larger Qwen in 4-bit on the same machine does not make the judge or the comparison usable. The
size of the model is not what limits these tasks as they are asked now. Whether the 26 stable
winners and the per-photo tops are the photos a photographer would pick still needs the user's
picks (`cull.py pick`); the answers of both models are saved for that.

Not tested: the full or FP8 27B (needs 48 GB, `g6e.xlarge`), another model family (Gemma 4 31B).
